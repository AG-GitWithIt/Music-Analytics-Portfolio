# Jukebox Product Strategy & Unit Economics Model

## Overview
This document details the financial architecture, unit economics, and behavioral retention strategy for **The Jukebox**—a high-margin, scarcity-based music membership model designed for a gated cohort of 5,000 "Superfans."

Unlike mass-market streaming platforms that rely on ad revenue and infinite background listening, The Jukebox operates as a gated collector’s club where music intentionality, gamification, and direct artist monetization drive platform profitability.

---

## 1. Unit Economics & Financial Engine

### Core Parameters
* **Target Cohort:** 5,000 High-Intent Members
* **Pricing Structure:** $5.00 per 20-credit pack ($0.25 per song play)
* **Baseline Cadence:** 12 packs/year ($60.00 annual spend per user)
* **Gross Annual Revenue Baseline:** **$300,000.00**

### Breakage & Royalty Split Architecture
* **Unspent Credit Breakage (12.0%):** **$36,000.00** captured as 100% net margin capital (zero royalty payout liability).
* **Net Active Working Revenue:** **$264,000.00** allocated to music plays.

## 2. Product Mechanics & Behavioral Retention Strategy

### The "Arcade Pinball" Curation Model
Every 20-credit pack is allocated across four intentional curation buckets:
1. **5 Comfort Tracks:** High-retention safety blanket catalog hits.
2. **5 Stretch Tracks:** Discovery tracks pushing users out of algorithmic bubbles.
3. **5 Active Reject Tracks:** Filter selections that gamify personal taste telemetry.
4. **5 Wildcard Tracks:** Unexpected masterpieces outside standard recommendation loops.

### Behavioral Mechanics & Persona Alignment

#### 1. Flexible Refill Cadence (Adaptive Crate Billing)
Replaces rigid monthly subscription dates. High-velocity users can pull $5 refills forward early, while low-velocity users retain unused credits without cancellation guilt.

#### 2. The "Bonus Ball" Pinball Mechanic
When a user burns 15+ credits within two weeks, the app triggers a targeted challenge (*"Play 2 Stretch tracks this week to unlock +3 Bonus Credits"*), boosting retention and accelerating refill velocity without diluting net margin.

#### 3. Low-Velocity Retention Mitigations
Prevents account churn through non-intrusive "Auto-Jukebox" trivia micro-games and 1-tap artist patronage gifting valves, maintaining a stable **12.0% breakage baseline ($36,000 pure margin)** while keeping churn near zero.
